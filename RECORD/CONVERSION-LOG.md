# CONVERSION LOG — mapped / inferred / undefined, per element, per stage

- **mapped** — came from the source.
- **inferred** — supplied by the grammar-served model, constrained by the grammar.
- **undefined** — product intent the source doesn't carry and the grammar can't supply; logged, ruled by Frank, never invented.

## A0

No external source enters at A0 (the app is generated from the alpha prompt alone). Log begins at A1.

## A1 — Figma catalog → VBL

### Rulings (Frank, 2026-09-20) — these govern every A1 entry below

1. **Data shape may be extended.** The design carries origin and mass as separate values;
   the A0 data carries one `variant` string. A1 may extend `data.products` with `origin`
   and `mass` keys (the model does it; it is logged). `variant` may be kept or dropped —
   the model decides and the log says which.
2. **Layer names are left as-is.** Price, origin, and mass text layers are named by their
   content (`$4,250`, `101955 BENNU`, `12.44g`), not by data keys. They are NOT renamed.
   The conversion has to infer the mapping from context; the log records those inferences
   as *inferred*, not *mapped*. This is the harder test, chosen deliberately.
3. **Provenance.** The frame was generated by Figma's in-file agent (not First Draft) from
   the prompt recorded verbatim in `prompts/A1-0-figma.md`.
